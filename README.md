

## CareCircle – Healthcare Coordination Platform

**CareCircle** is a web-based healthcare coordination platform designed to help senior citizens manage their daily healthcare needs while keeping their caregivers and family members informed. It addresses the challenges of medication mismanagement, missed appointments, lack of care coordination, and limited visibility into seniors' day-to-day wellbeing.

### Key Features
- **User Authentication & Profiles:** Secure signup, login, and personalized profiles.
- **Medication Management:** Add and manage medications, track doses, and monitor medication schedules.
- **Appointment Management:** Schedule and manage medical appointments and track their status.
- **Caregiver Connections:** Connect seniors with caregivers and family members through an invite-code-based system.
- **Care Alerts:** Generate alerts for overdue medication doses to help prevent missed medications.
- **Daily Wellbeing Check-ins:** Allow seniors to record their mood, sleep quality, appetite, and personal notes.
- **Caregiver Dashboard:** Enable connected caregivers and family members to view relevant medication, appointment, alert, and wellbeing information.

### Technologies, Tools & Platforms
- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript, Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase, PostgreSQL
- **Authentication:** Supabase Auth
- **Database Security:** Row Level Security (RLS)
- **Deployment:** Vercel
- **Development Tools:** Visual Studio Code, Git, GitHub, npm

### Impact
CareCircle brings essential healthcare coordination features into one platform, helping seniors stay organized and supporting caregivers and family members in providing more informed and consistent care.
